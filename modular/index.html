Modular, one take

Six voices played from one string. Chords by hand. No computer, no undo.

Also on YouTube

Drums, bass, chord swells, stabs and granular textures come from a small sequencer I wrote for the Ornate Criminal, a Eurorack module built around a Teensy. Each voice has a bank of patterns and three levels of intensity. One string controller sets how busy the drums and bass are and sweeps the effects; the chords are played by hand on a keyboard and harmonised in the case.

The audio is the direct recording from the mixer, lined up with the room video afterwards.

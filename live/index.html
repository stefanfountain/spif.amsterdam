№ 03 — What it is

The instrument is the room.

The crowd plays with fluorescent tubes, becoming part of the music: hold one up and it lights, drawing lightning from the coil. The coil is a speaker as well as a spectacle: the arc itself makes the sound, ionising the air into notes.
